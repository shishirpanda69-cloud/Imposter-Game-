# 🎭 Who Is The Imposter?

A fun multiplayer word-based social deduction game inspired by classic party games like "Who Is The Spy".

Players receive a secret word, except for one randomly selected player who becomes the **Imposter**.

The Imposter receives a small hint instead of the secret word and must bluff their way through the clue round without getting caught.

## 🎮 How To Play

1. Choose the number of players.
2. Select a category.
3. Choose the clue timer.
4. The game randomly selects an Imposter.
5. Pass the device to each player.
6. Each player secretly checks their role.
7. One player is randomly selected to start.
8. Players take turns giving one clue.
9. Everyone discusses the clues.
10. Vote for who you think is the Imposter.
11. If the Imposter is caught, they get one final chance to guess the secret word.
12. The winner is revealed.
